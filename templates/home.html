Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free to register?

Yes, farmer and buyer registration is free. The platform may charge a small configurable commission on completed transactions.

How do I get paid?

For the MVP, payments are arranged directly between buyer and farmer (cash on delivery, bank transfer, UPI). A secure payment gateway integration is planned.

Are the market prices live?

Market prices come from authorised APMC/AGMARKNET data when connected. We never invent live prices — if data is unavailable we say so and show the last known date.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. Identity documents are never shown publicly, passwords are securely hashed, and contact details are shared only with your consent.
