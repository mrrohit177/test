About FarmConnect

{{ SITE_TAGLINE }}

FarmConnect is an India-first digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with buyers — eliminating unnecessary intermediaries and building a transparent agricultural trading ecosystem.

A farmer in a small village can list produce, set their own price, receive enquiries and sell directly to a buyer in another city or state. Our initial focus is Maharashtra, with an architecture designed to expand across India.

Our Mission

To empower farmers with fair prices and give buyers direct access to fresh agricultural produce, with transparency at every step.
